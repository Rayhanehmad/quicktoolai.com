Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is BMI?

A: BMI (Body Mass Index) estimates body fat from weight and height.

Q: What is a healthy BMI range?

A: For adults, 18.5–24.9 is generally considered healthy.

Q: Is BMI accurate for athletes?

A: Athletes may have high BMI due to muscle mass; BMI doesn't distinguish muscle from fat.
