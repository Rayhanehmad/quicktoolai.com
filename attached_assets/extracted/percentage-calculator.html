Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How to find percent?

A: (part/whole)×100 gives the percent value.

Q: How to compute percent change?

A: ((new-old)/old)×100 produces percent change.

Q: Can I use negative values?

A: Yes—negative indicates decrease.
