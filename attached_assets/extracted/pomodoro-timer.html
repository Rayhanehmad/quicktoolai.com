Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Pomodoro?

A: A productivity technique using work intervals (commonly 25 minutes) followed by short breaks.

Q: Can I change durations?

A: Yes—set custom work and break times in minutes.

Q: Does it run when tab is inactive?
