Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is BMR?

A: BMR is calories burned at rest to maintain vital functions.

Q: Which formula is used?

A: We use Mifflin-St Jeor, widely accepted for estimates.

Q: Can I use BMR for diet planning?

A: Yes—multiply BMR by an activity factor to get daily needs.
