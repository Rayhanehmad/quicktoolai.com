Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is TDEE?

A: Total Daily Energy Expenditure is calories burned per day including activity.

Q: How to use this?

A: Compute BMR then multiply by an activity factor (1.2 sedentary to 1.9 very active).

Q: Is it precise?

A: It's an estimate; use it as a starting point and adjust with real-world results.
