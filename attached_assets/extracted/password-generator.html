Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are generated passwords secure?

A: Reasonably secure for general use; use longer length and varied symbols for higher security.

Q: Do you store passwords?

A: No—generation is client-side and never stored.

Q: Can I copy to clipboard?

A: Yes—add navigator.clipboard.writeText() to copy generated password.
